# IMPRIA Base: Free CRM for print shops and businesses that work with customer files

IMPRIA Base keeps customers, conversations, orders and working folders connected in one CRM on your local network. Install it on one Mac, then work on that computer or from Windows PCs and other Macs on the same network.

The CRM and Work Center interfaces are available in English, Spanish and Russian.

Base is free to use in your own business. There is no expiry date, software subscription or license-imposed limit on the number of orders.

## Built for a print shop

I'm Sergei Oseledchik. I ran a small print shop for many years and built IMPRIA around the workflow I knew.

A customer writes on WhatsApp or by email and sends a document or artwork. You need to confirm the specifications, agree on the price, prepare the files and complete the job. When that customer returns, you need to find the previous work and understand exactly what you did for them.

In a typical setup, conversations live in a messenger or mailbox, order details live in the CRM, and working files live in folders on your computers. Keeping those parts connected takes manual work.

In Base, each customer has a CRM record and a folder. Each order has its own card and a linked folder with the same number. The conversation, specifications, status and working files belong to the same order.

This approach can also help other small businesses whose work involves documents, images, drawings, models, audio or video. Base is primarily intended for independent professionals and small teams working in one office or workshop, typically up to about 15 people.

## What you can do with Base

- Keep customer records and their order history.
- Work with conversations from connected channels alongside order information.
- Create orders manually and move them through your workflow.
- Add order line items with specifications, comments and manually entered prices.
- Use automatically created customer and order folders linked to the CRM.
- Link working files and folders to orders.
- Open an order's folder in Finder or Windows File Explorer from its CRM card.
- Send selected files to a customer through a connected channel and continue the conversation in the CRM.

In Base, a person handles customer conversations. AI employees and automated workflows are part of the paid Pro version.

## Keep working files on your network

Large-format banner artwork, a layered PSD, a Blender project or source video footage can take up a lot of space. For day-to-day work, it is often more practical to open these files from a local drive or your office network.

Base does not require you to upload working files to a cloud CRM just to associate them with an order. Save them in the linked folder and open them in your usual software.

For example, your team can keep the large source file locally while sending the customer an exported preview for approval. The working file and the file used for discussion can be different, while both belong to the same order.

Open each format in the appropriate software: PDFs in a viewer or editor, PSDs in an image editor, DWG files in CAD software and BLEND files in Blender. Base organizes the work around those files; it does not replace these applications. Sending a particular file is subject to the limits of the connected channel.

## Example: a large banner order

A customer contacts you through connected WhatsApp or email. Open their customer record and create a banner order.

1. Enter the dimensions, material, quantity, comments and price.
2. Open the linked order folder and save the working materials there.
3. Prepare the artwork in your usual software.
4. Export a small preview and link it to the order.
5. Send the preview from the CRM conversation and receive the reply there.
6. Once the customer agrees, move the order to its next stage.

The large source file stays in the local working folder. Your team opens it over the network, while the conversation records what was agreed with the customer.

## Example: an architect discussing drawing revisions

You are working on a drawing in AutoCAD or another CAD application. The working project is in the order folder.

1. Export a PDF for the customer and save it alongside the project.
2. Link the PDF to the order and send it through a connected channel.
3. Receive feedback in the conversation and add an order comment where useful.
4. Open the working folder from the CRM card and continue editing in your own application.
5. Save the next version under a separate name and send it for discussion.

For example, you might use `plan-v01.pdf` and `plan-v02.pdf`. This is your own file organization: Base does not replace version control or a specialist CAD viewer.

## Example: approving an ad for a magazine or website

An advertising manager is discussing a newspaper or magazine ad with a customer. The designer lays out the page in InDesign, saves the working project in the order folder and exports a PDF. The customer receives the PDF through the CRM conversation and sees how their ad looks on the page alongside the other content.

Feedback and subsequent versions stay in that order's conversation. Use the same process for a web banner: send the banner itself or a prepared image showing its placement on a website page. Source files stay in the working folder, while the order keeps the versions sent out and the customer's approval reply together.

## Example: an animation studio sharing a new iteration

The Blender, After Effects or editing project stays in the order folder. Export a video preview for discussion.

1. Save the preview of the new iteration and link it to the order.
2. Send the selected file to the customer from the CRM.
3. Receive the reply in the same conversation and record the requested changes in comments.
4. Continue working on the source files in your usual application.

The CRM keeps the discussion in context, while the team accesses working materials over the local network. This workflow does not include frame-by-frame annotations or a dedicated review player.

## When a customer returns six months later

A customer wants another print run, a similar item or more work on an existing project. Find their record in the CRM and open the previous orders, conversations and linked folders.

The new order gets its own card and folder inside that customer's folder. Earlier materials remain available: you can check what you discussed, which specifications you used and which files you prepared.

This history is useful both for repeat business and when another team member takes over a job.

## Full working access from Windows and Mac

One Mac hosts the shared CRM. From a Windows PC or another Mac on the same local network, you can create and edit orders, work with customers and conversations, change statuses and open linked working folders.

Use a browser to access the CRM. To open local folders from a card, set up IMPRIA Workspace Opener and access to the shared folder on that workstation. The workstation installer and instructions are available in Work Center's files and workstations setup.

For example, a receptionist can take an order on Windows, a designer can work on its files on a Mac, and a production colleague can mark it as ready from another computer. You do not need a Mac for every employee.

Base uses the owner's account. Separate staff accounts with individual permissions are available in Pro. The reference to teams of about 15 people describes the intended audience, not a technical limit or the number of included accounts.

## A Mac mini as your CRM server

You can use a suitable Mac mini, including a used one: compatible models can be found for a few hundred dollars. Check the current installer's requirements before buying hardware.

Leave the Mac mini running in your office. macOS includes settings to prevent automatic sleep and start the computer after power is restored.

During installation, IMPRIA configures startup after you log in to macOS. You do not need to add it to login items separately; macOS may ask you to approve the setting.

With power and login settings configured, you can arrange for work to resume automatically after a power outage. If your security settings require a password after restarting, you will need to log in to macOS first.

You do not need to rent a cloud server to run the core CRM.

## Work without internet access

After initial installation and setup, you can use the local CRM without internet access: create and edit orders, change statuses and open working files.

If a customer brings a document on a USB drive, add it to an order and carry on working. Team computers still need a working local network, and the Mac running IMPRIA must be switched on.

While the internet is unavailable, external channels such as WhatsApp and email are unavailable too. When connectivity returns, IMPRIA reconnects to the channels and loads the messages retained during the interruption into the CRM.

Customer messaging, cloud synchronization of backups and downloading updates require internet access.

## Backups

Base includes optional backups. Choose a backup folder and enable a schedule.

If that folder is synchronized with your cloud storage, the installed cloud service application uploads the backup copies. The working database remains local; the cloud stores an additional copy.

You can use the latest successful backup when restoring on a compatible Mac. Keep backups separate from the working drive, and periodically check that they are being created and can be restored. Keep connection passwords and license keys separately: a working-data backup is not a substitute for them.

## Get started on one Mac

1. Open [impria.app](https://impria.app/) and choose Base.
2. Choose the Intel or Apple Silicon installer and check that release's system requirements.
3. Install the application and open Work Center, IMPRIA's setup interface.
4. Complete initial setup: language, the owner's personal recovery email, regional settings, the Workspace folder and CRM access.
5. Open the CRM from Work Center and create your first manual order.
6. Open that order's folder, add a test file and try your usual workflow.
7. Connect the customer channels you need. WhatsApp uses QR device pairing; email requires incoming and outgoing connection settings.
8. Check an incoming message from another account and confirm that a manual reply from the CRM arrives.

You can start working with manual orders before connecting every channel. Treat your personal recovery email and customer-facing business mailbox as separate purposes, even if the same person manages both.

## Connect other computers

1. Make sure the Mac running IMPRIA and your workstations are on the same local network.
2. Open files and workstations setup in Work Center.
3. Follow the instructions for Windows or macOS. Set up access to the shared working folder and install Workspace Opener.
4. Open the shared CRM in the workstation's browser using the installed Mac's local address.
5. Check that you can create or edit a test order and open its folder from the card.

Each computer does not get a separate database: everyone works with the same CRM and shared working folders. Windows and macOS instructions differ; use the kit supplied by your installation.

## Frequently asked questions

### Is Base really free for business use?

Yes. You can use Base in your own business for free, with no expiry date or license-imposed order limit. You do not need to buy Pro or make a donation to keep using Base. Hardware and third-party provider services are not included with the free application.

### Does every employee need a Mac?

No. One compatible Mac runs the main system. Staff can use the shared CRM and files from Windows PCs and other Macs on the same network.

### Can I install the main system on Windows?

The packaged IMPRIA installer is for Mac. Windows is supported as a workstation for accessing the CRM and shared files over the local network.

### Do I have to upload source files to the cloud?

Not to associate working files with orders or work together over your local network. If you send a file to a customer through an external channel or enable cloud synchronization of backups, the relevant data passes through your chosen service.

### Can I work with Photoshop, Illustrator, AutoCAD and Blender?

Keep working files in order folders and open them in the applications installed on your computers. To send something to a customer, choose a suitable source file or exported preview within the channel's capabilities. The CRM does not provide built-in editing for all these formats.

### Is there automatic version history or a separate customer approval portal?

The examples above use conversations for approval and separate filenames for different versions. They do not imply a separate customer portal, automatic version control or a specialist review tool.

### Does free Base include AI employees?

Base is for manual work with customers, orders and files. AI employees and the personal AI assistant are part of Pro. Your own external AI assistant can help you understand Base's documentation and setup.

Start with Base and try your normal working process. Upgrade to Pro from Work Center in the installed application.
